Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 30, 2026

The short version: Bentrix does not collect your personal information. Your investment records stay in the app’s local storage on your iPhone. There is no Bentrix account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or connection to a Bentrix server.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the Bentrix iPhone app (“Bentrix” or the “app”) handles information. It also describes the limited information involved when you contact support or visit this website.

2. Information you enter in Bentrix

You may enter deal, pipeline, portfolio, company, financial, diligence, and activity information in the app. You may also attach photographs or supporting documents to your records.

This content is stored locally on your iPhone. Bentrix does not send this content to us, sync it to a Bentrix account, or make it accessible to us. The app makes no network requests.

3. Information we do not collect

Through the app, we do not collect or receive:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials;
  • Your deal, portfolio, financial, or company records;
  • Precise or approximate location data;
  • Contacts, calendars, or health data;
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking data;
  • Usage analytics, behavioral profiles, or advertising data; or
  • Crash reports through any third-party analytics service included by us.

Bentrix contains no third-party advertising or analytics software development kits and does not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising.

4. Camera and photo library access

Bentrix requests access to device features only for actions you choose:

  • Camera: to photograph site visits, contracts, and diligence evidence for attachment to your records.
  • Photo library — read access: to add images you select to your records.
  • Photo library — add access: to save copies of evidence photos when you explicitly choose to save them.

Photos and documents selected or created for your records are processed and stored on your device. You can change permissions at any time in your iPhone settings. Denying access does not prevent you from using features that do not require the camera or photo library.

5. Exports and sharing

You can choose to export workspace information as CSV files, save evidence photos, or share exported content using iOS. These actions occur only at your request. Once you export or share content, it is governed by the apps, services, and recipients you choose. Bentrix does not receive a copy of your exports.

6. Device backups

Depending on your device and Apple settings, locally stored app data may be included in backups you make through Apple. Bentrix does not control those backups. Apple’s handling of backup data is governed by your agreement with Apple and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

7. Support communications

If you email lueilwitzmarion1262000@gmail.com, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use this information only to respond, provide support, troubleshoot issues, and maintain the security and quality of the app.

Please do not send passwords, confidential deal documents, financial account details, or other sensitive information that is not necessary for support. Support email is delivered through your email provider and ours; their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

8. This website

This informational website does not use cookies, advertising, analytics, or tracking scripts. It is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may process standard technical information, such as your IP address and request logs, to deliver and protect the website. This processing is subject to GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

9. Data retention and deletion

Your app records remain on your device until you edit or delete them, reset the workspace, or delete Bentrix. A workspace reset permanently deletes locally stored app records. Exported files, saved photos, and device backups are separate copies and must be managed or deleted through the services where they are stored.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain support records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

10. Security

Because app records are stored locally, protecting your iPhone is important. Use a device passcode, keep iOS up to date, manage backups carefully, and be selective when exporting or sharing records. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Children’s privacy

Bentrix is a business and financial record-keeping tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect user information, we generally have no way to identify a user’s age.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app or our practices change. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the date above. Material changes may also be communicated within the app or through an app update where appropriate.
