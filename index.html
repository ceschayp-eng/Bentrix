From first look to follow-on

A focused workspace for every stage.

Keep the numbers, evidence, and decisions behind your investment work together. Every chart and scenario is calculated from records you enter.

01 / OVERVIEW

See what needs attention

Review pipeline value, weighted opportunities, deployed capital, ownership value, sector exposure, runway, and upcoming next steps at a glance.

02 / PIPELINE

Move deals forward

Track each opportunity from sourcing through a term sheet. Keep the thesis, risks, check size, valuation, diligence checklist, documents, and site photos together.

03 / PORTFOLIO

Follow the companies you back

Monitor revenue run rate, burn, cash runway, headcount, cap table, milestones, ownership, and cost basis in one company record.

04 / ANALYSIS

Interrogate the numbers

Explore deployment, stage distribution, growth against valuation, and runway. Adjust exit multiples, growth rates, and hold periods to compare scenarios.

Your work stays yours

No account. No ads. No tracking.

Bentrix stores your workspace on your iPhone and makes no network requests. Camera and photo access are used only when you choose to add evidence to your records. CSV exports and photo saves happen only at your request.

Read the privacy policy

Bentrix is a record-keeping and analysis tool. It does not provide investment advice, execute investments, connect to financial institutions, or hold money. Figures are calculations based on information you enter. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
