Frequently asked questions

Which devices support Bentrix?

Bentrix is designed for iPhone and requires iOS 16.0 or later.

Do I need an account or subscription?

No. Bentrix has no account, sign-in, or subscription. Your workspace is stored locally on your iPhone.

Where is my information stored?

Your deal records, portfolio information, attached evidence, and workspace settings are stored on your iPhone. Bentrix does not send them to a developer server and makes no network requests. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I back up or move my workspace?

Use the app’s CSV export feature to create copies of supported workspace information. You can also save evidence photos individually. Keep exported files in a secure location. Device backups may also be available through Apple, depending on your iPhone settings. Bentrix does not provide cloud sync or its own backup service.

How do I export my data?

Open the app’s data management or export controls, choose the CSV export option, and select a destination in the iOS share sheet. Exported files are separate from the app; deleting Bentrix does not delete copies you saved elsewhere.

Why can’t Bentrix access my camera or photos?

Open your iPhone Settings, find Bentrix, and allow the camera or photo access you want to use. You can also review Camera and Photos under Settings > Privacy & Security. Bentrix uses these permissions only when you choose to attach or save evidence.

What does “Reset Workspace” do?

Reset Workspace permanently deletes the records stored by Bentrix on your iPhone. This action cannot be undone within the app. Export anything you need before resetting. Saved photos, exported files, and separate device backups are not necessarily removed by a workspace reset.

Can Bentrix recover deleted records?

No. Because records are local and there is no Bentrix account or cloud backup, we cannot recover deleted records. Recovery may only be possible from your own eligible device backup. Export important information regularly.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting Bentrix normally deletes its locally stored app data. Export any information you need first. Deleting the app does not remove CSV files, saved photos, or other copies you stored outside Bentrix.

Does Bentrix provide investment advice or execute trades?

No. Bentrix is a record-keeping and analysis tool. It does not provide financial, investment, legal, or tax advice; recommend investments; execute transactions; connect to financial institutions; or hold money. Calculations depend on the information and assumptions you enter.

Before reporting a calculation issue

Check the values, dates, ownership percentages, currency settings, and scenario assumptions you entered. If something still looks wrong, email us a description of the expected and actual results and the steps to reproduce them. Please use fictional or redacted values where possible.
